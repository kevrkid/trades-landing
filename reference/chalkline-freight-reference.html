Common questions

How far do you go?

Same-day across the Texas Triangle: Austin, Taylor, Bastrop, Round Rock, Georgetown, San Antonio, Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, and Sherman. Abilene, West Texas, and South Texas by arrangement.

How fast can you get here?

Call or text any day, including early starts, and you'll get a pickup time when you book. Hot shot runs go straight from pickup to your site.

What fits in the van?

Up to 3 pallets or 2,300 lb, with 488 cubic feet of space and 79 inches of standing height. Hazardous materials aren't carried.

Is there a trip fee?

No trip fee within 30 miles. Beyond that, a $75 trip fee, confirmed before booking.

How do I pay?

Card, cash, or Zelle.
